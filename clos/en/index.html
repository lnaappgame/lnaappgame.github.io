2. What data is processed?

2.1 Game data, on your device only

The Golden Stone Estate does not ask for an account, a name, an email address or a nickname to play. Your progress (board, storage and items, estate renovations, chapters and mysteries, story episodes and your answers, letters received, album, coins, gems, stars and energy, orders, market and reputation with the villagers, events such as Christmas at the Clos, purchases already delivered, language and evening reminder) is saved only on your device and is not sent to any server. If you uninstall the app or clear its data from your phone settings, this data is deleted.

Evening reminder. If you accept it (you are asked only once, and there is a switch in the game settings), the game schedules a reminder on your phone in the evening, at most one a day, and only if you have not played that day. Everything happens on your device: no data is sent for this. You can turn it off at any time, in the game or phone settings.

2.2 Analytics, technical diagnostics and remote settings (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to measure general usage of the game (for example: a renovation completed, an order delivered, a mystery solved, an answer in the story, a video watched, a purchase with its product, price and currency), Firebase Crashlytics to receive an automatic technical report when the app crashes (type of error, technical state of the app at the time of the incident) and Firebase Remote Config to receive the game's settings (for example how fast energy refills, order prices or video rewards) without publishing a new version. These tools, provided by Google, use technical data about your device (model, operating system version, installation identifier) but are not intended to identify you personally.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics is switched off when the app starts and is only enabled if you accept, in the consent tool described in section 2.3, the purposes “store and/or access information on a device” and “measure content performance”. Crash reports and remote settings, which the game needs to work properly, do not depend on this choice.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: rewarded videos that you freely choose to watch (for example to refill your energy, double the coins of an order, get a mystery hint or open a special market) and, from time to time, a full-screen ad, shown only right after an estate renovation, never in the middle of an action. The “No ads” option (section 2.4) removes these full-screen ads; rewarded videos remain optional. To serve, measure and personalize these ads, Google may collect technical data about your device: advertising identifier (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS — the latter only being accessible after your explicit permission through Apple's “App Tracking Transparency” prompt), approximate IP address, device information and interactions with ads.

LNA App does not receive or store this data itself: it is processed directly by Google, under its own privacy policy, available here: policies.google.com/privacy

A consent tool (Google User Messaging Platform, compliant with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework) is shown to users located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland before any ad is requested, in accordance with the GDPR. You can review your choices at any time from the game settings (“Privacy options”).

2.4 In-app purchases (Google Play / App Store)

The app offers optional in-app purchases: packs of gems, the welcome basket (once only, with Augustin's lantern), the Christmas basket during the Christmas at the Clos event (once only, with Lea's santon) and the “No ads” option. Both baskets and “No ads” are permanent purchases, which “Restore purchases” gives back to you (the gems and energy in the baskets are given only once, at the time of purchase). These purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or Apple's App Store: LNA App never sees or stores your payment information (card number, etc.). The app only receives the confirmation of the purchase, to unlock the corresponding content, and keeps the identifiers of the latest delivered purchases on your device, so that the same purchase is never delivered twice. Like the other game events, the purchase is also measured by Firebase Analytics, under the conditions of section 2.2: the product, its price and currency, without any payment information.

2.5 Test feedback (test version only)

During the closed test on Google Play, the test version of The Golden Stone Estate offers a “Testers” entry where you can give us your opinion or report a bug. Nothing is sent until you tap “Send”. A submission contains: your answers and texts; the first name or nickname you choose to give (optional); the app language; its version; the system version and your phone model (for example “OnePlus LE2113”); the number of days and sessions since installation; your progress in the game (for example “ch. 2, 3 rénovations”, that is chapter 2, 3 renovations); a randomly generated installation identifier, which links your submissions together without identifying you. This feedback is used only to improve the game. It is hosted by Google (Firebase Cloud Firestore) in the European Union, and only LNA App reads it. Please avoid writing personal information (address, phone number) in it. The public version of The Golden Stone Estate does not contain this entry.

“Thanks to our testers” screen. In the “Testers” entry, an optional box, unchecked by default, lets you ask for your first name or nickname to appear in the game’s “Thanks to our testers” screen, which is also in the public version. If you check it, your submission records that choice and we add your nickname to the displayed list, which the app receives through Firebase Remote Config. Only that nickname is shown, never any other information. You can ask us to remove it at any time (section 11).

2.6 What we do not do

LNA App does not sell any data to third parties and does not use any analytics or ad-targeting tool other than those listed above (Google AdMob, Firebase). The Golden Stone Estate currently offers no online leaderboard and no exchange between players: your progress is not sent to any server. The app has no access to your contacts, photos, precise location or any other data on your device not mentioned in this policy.

Other games from the studio. At most once a day, the app downloads the list of the other LNA App games from our website (lnaappgame.github.io, hosted by GitHub), to show them to you from time to time. As with any web page, GitHub receives your device's IP address; nothing else is sent. If you tap one of these games, its Google Play or App Store page opens with a link that only says it comes from The Golden Stone Estate. From time to time, the game may also show the Google Play or App Store rating window: your rating goes straight to the store.

5. Description of the Service

The Golden Stone Estate is a merge and mystery game set in Provence: the User merges items on a board to fill the villagers' orders, earns stars and gradually renovates an estate, chapter after chapter, while investigating its secrets. The App is available in French and English.

The App notably offers:

  • Board and orders: items to merge, produced by sources that use energy, and the villagers' orders.
  • Renovations and chapters: the estate changes as stars are earned; each chapter tells a story and ends with a mystery to solve.
  • Market and reputation: a stall to sell produce, villagers whose opinion changes and, depending on the chapter, a grape harvest, recipes and a scent workshop.
  • Story: episodes with choices, letters and appointments with the villagers; the User's answers change their bonds with them and how the story continues. The whole story can be played without paying anything; optional bonus scenes are obtained with gems, at a displayed price, with no randomness.
  • Daily visits and events: a daily reward and daily tasks, a free gift box every few hours, an album to complete, and time-limited events such as Christmas at the Clos in December.
  • Shop: energy and gems, earned by playing, through optional videos and through optional In-App Purchases (section 10).

The App offers energy, gems and boosts (mystery hints, a free gift box) obtained by playing or by watching optional videos, as well as optional In-App Purchases described in section 10.

This description is provided for information; the Publisher reserves the right to change, add or remove features under the conditions of section 18.
