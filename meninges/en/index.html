2. What data is processed?

2.1 Game data, on your device only

Daily Brain Gym does not ask for an account, a name, an email address or a nickname to play. Your progress (daily challenges completed, day streak and catch-up coffees, Stars, weekly goals, brain shape for each skill, difficulty level of each game, blitz records, grids in your gallery) is stored only on your device and is not sent to any server. If you uninstall the app or clear its data from your phone's settings, this data is deleted.

2.2 Analytics, technical diagnostics and remote settings (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to measure general use of the game (for example: a challenge started or completed, its duration, a hint used, a grid completed, a video watched), Firebase Crashlytics to receive an automatic technical report if the app crashes (type of error, technical state of the app at the time) and Firebase Remote Config to receive the game's settings (for example the number of Stars earned or the number of videos offered) without publishing a new version. These tools, provided by Google, use technical data about your device (model, operating system version, installation identifier) but are not intended to identify you personally.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics is turned off when the app starts and is only turned on if you accept, in the consent tool described in section 2.3, the purposes “store and/or access information on a device” (purpose 1) and “measure content performance” (purpose 8). Crash reports and remote settings, which the game needs to work properly, do not depend on this choice.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: mainly videos you freely choose to watch in exchange for a benefit (a hint, a second attempt at the daily challenge, doubled Stars) and, where applicable, ads between two free-play games (never during a game or during the daily challenge). To serve, measure and personalise these ads, Google may collect technical data about your device: advertising identifier (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS — the latter only after your explicit permission through Apple's “App Tracking Transparency” prompt), approximate IP address, device information and interactions with ads.

LNA App neither receives nor stores this data itself: it is processed directly by Google, under its own privacy policy, available here: policies.google.com/privacy

A consent tool (Google User Messaging Platform, compliant with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework) is shown to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland before any ad request, in accordance with the GDPR. You can review your choices at any time from the app's home screen (“Privacy options”).

2.4 In-app purchases (Google Play / App Store)

The app may offer optional in-app purchases (see the Terms of Use). These purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or Apple's App Store: LNA App never sees or keeps your payment information (card number, etc.). Only the confirmation that a purchase was made is sent to the app, to unlock the corresponding content.

2.5 What we do not do

LNA App does not sell any data to third parties and does not use any analytics or ad-targeting tool other than those listed above (Google AdMob, Firebase). The app has no access to your contacts, your photos, your precise location or any other data on your device not mentioned in this policy.

Worldwide ranking (coming later). A future version will let you see how you compare with other players in each skill. Today, no game result is sent to our servers. Before this ranking goes live, this policy will be completed to describe precisely the data published (anonymously), its purpose and its retention period.

2.6 Test feedback (test version only)

During the test phase, the version distributed to testers has a “Testers” tab that lets you, entirely voluntarily, send us your opinion or report a bug. A submission contains: your answers to the questionnaire and the texts you write, the first name or nickname you choose to give (optional), your device model, the system and app versions, the language, the number of days since installation and of sessions, your progress in the game (for example “12 daily challenges”) and a randomly generated installation identifier, which links your submissions together without identifying you. This feedback is used only to improve the game. It is received by LNA App through Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), as a processor, and deleted after at most 12 months. Please avoid writing personal details (address, phone number) in the text boxes. This tab does not exist in the public version.

5. Description of the Service

Daily Brain Gym is a daily logic, memory and arithmetic game: each day, a short challenge (a few minutes) works on a different skill. The App is available in French and English.

The App offers in particular:

  • Daily challenge: each day of the week, a different format (logic sequence, hidden pairs, everyday arithmetic, mystery grid, gentle speed, surprise…), the same for all players on the same date, with a streak of consecutive days that “catch-up coffees” can protect from a missed day.
  • Free play: mystery grids to collect in a gallery and 60-second blitz rounds, with a personal record.
  • Brain shape: a pentagon showing the User's level in five skills, based on their latest games, and weekly goals.

The App offers hints and other benefits obtained with the Stars earned by playing or by watching optional ads, as well as, where applicable, the optional In-App Purchases described in section 10.
